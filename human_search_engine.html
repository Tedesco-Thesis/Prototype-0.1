PROTOTYPE 03 — THE HUMAN SEARCH ENGINE

who might know?

what changes when finding information becomes an encounter with someone else's uncertain, sometimes conflicting experience? try "old websites" — you'll get two people who disagree.
real search engines return pages ranked by relevance. this returns people — uncertain, sometimes wrong, sometimes disagreeing with each other.
what this prototype is testing
QUESTION: what changes when finding information becomes an encounter with another person's experience, rather than an extraction of information from a page?
INTERVENTION: results are people, not documents — they can be uncertain, disagree with each other, or admit they're not the right person to ask.
TRADEOFF: this is slower and less authoritative than a ranked answer, and it doesn't converge on one "correct" result the way search normally promises to.
OBSERVATION: notice what happens when Mina and Jon disagree — do you trust one more than the other, and on what basis, given you have no way to verify either?
LIMITATION: the "people" here are scripted, not real — a genuine version of this would need real, consenting, differently-informed people, which raises its own availability and labor questions this prototype doesn't address.
