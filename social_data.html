what this prototype is testing
QUESTION: can data represent a relationship without reducing it to a metric — and is the qualitative version automatically better?
INTERVENTION: the exact same logged events are shown two ways: as specific remembered moments, and as a standard engagement dashboard.
TRADEOFF: the human log is more specific and harder to compare across relationships; the metrics panel is instantly comparable and completely illegible about what actually happened.
OBSERVATION: notice which view you check first after logging a few events, and whether the "engagement score" changes how the relationship feels even though nothing about it actually changed.
LIMITATION: both views here are equally fabricated by this prototype — a real version would need to reckon with which one a platform actually has an incentive to show you by default.
