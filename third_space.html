about.htm

what this is

a working prototype of what an internet built around reciprocity, instead of optimization, might feel like. every room refuses to make itself easier in a different way — the data room asks for something back, the correspondence room asks for time and commitment, and the signed-work room asks for visible labor instead of a market.

this is a thesis prototype, not a finished product. it's meant to be felt, not just read about.
