what this prototype is testing
QUESTION: what kinds of friction are meaningful, and what kinds are merely inconvenient?
INTERVENTION: the same task is completed under three conditions — instant/ranked, seamful (uncertainty and alternatives shown), and deliberately obscure/dead-ended.
TRADEOFF: optimized mode is fastest and least memorable; unoptimized mode is slowest and most likely to produce an incidental discovery or a genuine feeling of tedium — it can go either way.
OBSERVATION: watch whether people tag friction as "focused/discovery" or "tedious" — and whether that changes if they've already done the task once in another mode.
LIMITATION: a one-time toy search task can't capture what friction does over repeated, long-term use — real seamfulness or difficulty is felt over weeks, not one click-through.
