the machine here isn't obviously bad — it can make you clearer, kinder, more confident. if it does, what happens to the hesitation and authorship that were part of the original?

what this prototype is testing
QUESTION: if AI mediation makes communication clearer, kinder, and more effective, what happens to the ambiguity, hesitation, and authorship of the original message?
INTERVENTION: the machine's edits are shown in full before sending — including what it removed, what it inferred about your emotional state, and what you specifically give up by accepting.
TRADEOFF: the machine's version is usually more legible and less likely to be misread — at the cost of the hesitation or roughness that may have been part of what you actually meant.
OBSERVATION: watch your own accept/reject pattern over several messages — do you accept more often as the conversation goes on, and do you notice when you stop reading the intercept panel closely?
LIMITATION: this machine is honest about its edits by design — a real deployed system has no incentive to show you what it removed, which is itself a finding worth sitting with.
